Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery hours?

Our express delivery slots operate daily from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM. You can choose 30–60 minute express delivery or schedule convenient morning/evening slots.

How does the return policy work?

We offer a 100% "No Questions Asked" instant refund or replacement at the doorstep if you are unsatisfied with any fresh produce item.

How do I get Free Delivery?

All orders with a subtotal of ₹499 and above automatically qualify for 100% free doorstep delivery. For orders below ₹499, a nominal delivery fee of ₹49 applies.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI, Visa, MasterCard, RuPay debit/credit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
