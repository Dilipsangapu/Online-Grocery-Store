How It All Began

From Local Farm Orchards Straight to Your Doorstep

Traditional grocery supply chains involve 4–5 intermediaries, causing produce to sit on trucks for days before reaching customers. We bypassed the middlemen by setting up direct farm-gate collection hubs in Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Maharashtra.

Our temperature-monitored micro-fulfillment dark stores ensure that your leafy greens, stone fruits, dairy pouches, and cold-pressed staples reach you at peak freshness with zero nutrient degradation.
